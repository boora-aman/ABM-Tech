### Day 9 · Tuesday — Tech Tuesday

**[A] Carousel, 5 slides — "Why your payment gateway can double-charge"**

> **Hook:** A customer paid twice. The gateway says once. Who's wrong?

**Caption:**
This is the bug almost every first e-commerce build has, and it's invisible until it costs you.

**How it breaks:** the browser redirects back after payment and *that redirect* is what creates the order. If the customer's network drops at that exact moment — or they refresh — the order either never gets created, or gets created twice.

**How it should work:** the order is created from the gateway's own server-to-server webhook, with an idempotency key. Same payment fires the webhook five times? Exactly one order. Customer closes the tab mid-payment? The order still lands.

Ask whoever built your store one question: *"is the order created from the redirect or from the webhook?"*

Their answer tells you everything.
